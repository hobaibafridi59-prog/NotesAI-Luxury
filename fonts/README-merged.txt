NotesAIMerged-Regular.ttf and NotesAIMerged-Bold.ttf are generated by merging
the bundled Noto Sans and Noto Naskh Arabic font files with fontTools.

They are distributed under the same SIL Open Font License terms as the source
Noto fonts. See OFL-NotoSans.txt and OFL-NotoNaskhArabic.txt in this directory.

The merged family exists so one PDF text cell can contain both Latin and
Arabic/Urdu glyphs. This avoids fpdf2's fallback-font/colour glyph corruption
that can otherwise reverse or replace English text after Urdu text.
